# B NET Pitch Guide — TechVerse ISP Digital Platform Demo

## Positioning

Do not say: "We built a new B NET website."

Say: "We built a small ISP digital-service concept to show how customer acquisition, coverage checks, installation requests and support could work in one customer journey. We want to understand how B NET handles these processes today before recommending a production solution."

## Recommended live demo sequence

1. **Home** — show the customer journey and explain that the interface is a concept, not a commissioned B NET product.
2. **Plans** — show how customers can compare packages without calling sales.
3. **Coverage** — show the serviceability step before installation.
4. **Installation** — submit a mock installation request and explain the operations handoff.
5. **Support** — create a mock ticket and show the operations queue.
6. **Portal** — show how an existing customer can view plan, bill, service status and support.

## Discovery questions to ask B NET

- How do customers currently discover your packages?
- How do customers request a new installation?
- How do you confirm serviceability at a customer's location?
- How are outages and technical complaints currently recorded?
- How do customers see invoices or account information?
- Do business customers manage multiple connections anywhere today?
- Which customer requests still arrive mainly through phone or WhatsApp?
- Which support tasks take the most staff time?
- Do you already have a customer portal or internal ticketing system?
- Who owns these systems internally and what part is handled by external vendors?

## The goal of the first meeting

The goal is **not** to close a website project on the spot.

The goal is to identify one measurable operational/customer problem where a small paid pilot is sensible.

Example pilot outcomes:

- Installation request portal
- Customer support/ticketing front end
- Coverage checker
- Customer self-service portal
- Business customer dashboard
- Website + plan catalogue + lead capture

## Important caveat

All sample plans, prices, customer names, coverage, ticket IDs and operational data in the demo are illustrative.
